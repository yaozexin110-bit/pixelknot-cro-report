Why you'll love this kit?
Shape, and style your own homeware from vases to candle holders. Each kit includes premium air-dry clay and everything you need to create and finish your pieces. No kiln, no fuss, just pure creative flow.
4lbs of clay - enough for 4-6 creations (perfect for two!)
No kiln needed - air dries hard & strong, ready to paint in 48 hours
Tools, white paint & your choice of matte or glossy varnish
Add a paint set for even more color (optional, but highly recommended!)
Illustrated 36-page starter guide to get you going
A thoughtful gift for creatives, couples & curious minds
What's Inside the Kit - Earth Tones
Our soft, premium clay dries a smooth light grey, and is an absolute dream to work with.
4lbs Premium Sculpd Air-Dry Clay
8 Color Acrylic Earth Tones Paint Set
Pottery Carving, Shaping and Cutting Tools & Tool Bag
White Acrylic Eggshell Paint
Waterproof Glossy Varnish
2 Ultra Fine Bristle Paintbrushes and Potter’s Sponge for Smoothing
36 page step-by-step beginner's pottery guide, packed full of starter creations and inspiration
How to use
1
Prepare your space: lay out your clay & tools
2
Get to know your clay: knead & warm it up
3
Shape your pieces: mould your clay into your design
4
Add details & personalize: carve, stamp, or add texture
5
Leave to dry: 48hrs before painting
6
Add colors & designs: bring your piece to life with paint

Got Questions?
Who is this kit for?
The Sculpd Pottery Kit is perfect for anyone who loves getting creative, from first-time makers to seasoned artists. It’s a thoughtful gift for birthdays, date nights, or cozy weekends at home.
This product is intended for adults and children over 14 years old. If you are looking for products for children, please check out our
Kids range
.
How does the clay harden, and how should I store it?
Our air-dry clay naturally hardens within 24–48 hours - no kiln needed! It contains special fibers that let it dry strong and smooth. To keep leftovers soft, store them in the resealable bag and avoid leaving the clay out too long, as it will begin to dry. Once finished, add a coat of Sculpd varnish for a durable, ceramic-like finish.
How long does it take to ship?
Shipping times depend on the delivery option you choose at checkout.
Standard shipping typically arrives within 2–3 days for most contiguous US states.
Need it sooner? Choose Express 2 Day or Express 3 Day to guarantee faster delivery.
Can I return if I change my mind?
No problem - just get in touch and we’ll guide you through the returns process.
Please don’t send your kit to our registered address, as we won’t be able to receive or refund it there. Once your return reaches us, we’ll process your refund within 14 days.
