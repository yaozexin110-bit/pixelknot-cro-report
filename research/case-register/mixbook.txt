How do you make a custom photo book online? You can make a custom photo book online using Mixbook Studio, which uses AI technology to build your layout in three steps. First, choose a free theme, then upload your photos, and finally arrange your images or let the Auto-Create tool arrange them before you add custom text or stickers. Mixbook allows you to create books up to 400 pages, which is almost double the amount of competitor photo books. What's new in the Mixbook app? The new Mixbook app gives you more ways to make something meaningful from your photos. Create personalized art, movies, magazines, and photo books, relive your favorite moments as digital stories with captions and a timeline, and easily share your creations or turn them into something you can gift. It’s a whole new way to experience the moments in your camera roll. Learn more. What is Story Mode? Story Mode is Mixbook's AI-powered photo book creator. Simply answer a few questions, upload your photos, and Story Mode will automatically design a personalized photo book based on your memories. It organizes your photos, creates layouts, writes optional captions, and generates three unique book versions for you to choose from. You can order your book as-is or customize every detail before printing. Story Mode is now available everywhere! Use it on the web and in the free Mixbook mobile app for iPhone and iPad (iOS). If you have an Android device, you can join our waitlist to receive updates when an Android app is available. Learn more about the mobile app. How much does a photo book cost? You can't put a price on your treasured memories, but you can work within a budget that's comfortable for you. Price points vary with cover and paper types, book sizes, and the number of pages, but we have options to fit any budget. Learn more about our photo book pricing and start creating your dream photo book today! Can I create my own photo book layouts? Mixbook allows you to customize the layout of your photo book so you get it just the way you want it. You can choose different page layouts after uploading your photos. For example, you can change the background design/color and add multiple photos on a single page. I have so many photos. Is there an easy way to sort them all? Our "Sort by" feature makes it super easy to find the photos you want for your photo book. You can sort your photos by "Date Taken,""Date Added," or "File Name." To help you organize your photos even further, you can also "Hide Used" photos to ensure you aren't duplicating anything. Want to find a specific photo? Use the search feature and type in a key word to locate it. Can multiple people collaborate on a photo book online? Yes, multiple people can collaborate on a single Mixbook photo book online by inviting friends or family members via email. The project creator can manage permissions, allowing contributors to edit the design, add their own photos, view the project, or order their own printed copies.
