A little creativity, one sparkling facet at a time. Explore the craft Close the guide Diamond painting is the craft of setting tiny 100% resin diamonds onto a velvety canvas, one sparkling facet at a time. You place each diamond on a printed symbol, and the finished piece can be framed, hung, or gifted. This luminous craft is also called diamond art. 01 Prepare Prepare your canvas and diamond drills After opening your diamond painting kit, you will see all the supplies needed to complete the diamond painting. Next, peel back a small section of the clear film to reveal the adhesive, keeping the rest of your canvas covered. 02 Match Find the symbol on your canvas that matches a specific diamond color Find the bag with the matching color code, and carefully pour a few diamonds into your tray. Gently shake the tray back and forth to help the diamonds spread out and settle with faceted side up. 03 Place Place your diamonds and watch your picture sparkle Press the diamond onto its matching symbol and work one small section at a time as your picture takes shape. When you pause, cover the canvas and return unused diamonds to their labeled bags. One diamond at a time. Something beautiful. How it works Back in stock A fresh chance to find a design you love. Round diamonds leave small spaces between them for a softly textured, sparkling finish. Square diamonds fit snugly edge to edge, helping neighboring diamonds guide one another into alignment. Some people find that natural fit easier, with less precise positioning needed as a section fills. Both shapes are a great place to start. Choose the artwork and finish you love. Check the diamond shape on the product page. Do I need to start with a small canvas? No. Every canvas size uses the same technique: match each diamond to the symbol on the canvas. Larger designs have more diamonds to place. Choose artwork you love, check its dimensions, and work at your own pace. What comes with a diamond painting kit? Check the “What’s Included” section on each product page for the exact canvas, diamonds, and tools in that kit. Contents vary by design and format, so you can see what comes in the box before you order. Can I work on my kit a little at a time? Yes. Work on one section at a time and cover unfinished adhesive with its protective film when you pause. Pick it up again whenever you like; there is no need to finish in one sitting.
