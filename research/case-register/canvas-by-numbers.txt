Start Painting Today Frequently Asked Questions What is a paint by number kit? A paint by number kit is a painting set with a canvas that is pre-printed with outlined, numbered sections, plus acrylic paints numbered to match and a set of brushes. You paint each section in the color with the same number, and the picture comes together one area at a time. No drawing or color mixing needed. How long does it take to complete a paint by numbers for adults canvas? Generally, it depends on the canvas size and detail and your painting habits.
