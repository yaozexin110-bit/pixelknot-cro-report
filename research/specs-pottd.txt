What’s in the box?

No experience? No problem. With Pott’d, anyone can dive into the world of clay.
🎨 The Creative Kit is your go-to for fun, freestyle pottery. From stylish vases to cute trinket dishes (or something totally unexpected), this kit is all about creative freedom. You’ll get a quick-start guide and inspiration cards to spark ideas, plus all the essentials you need to bring your creations to life. It’s the perfect way to jump in, experiment, and enjoy pottery without any rules.
✨ Want to explore more? Upgrade to The Ultimate Starter Kit for a guided journey into clay. You’ll learn to make three classic pottery projects, each one building in difficulty while teaching you hand-building techniques, textures, decorations, and painting tips. With step-by-step instructions at every stage, it’s designed to help you grow your skills while creating pieces you’ll love to keep.
Whether you’re crafting solo for mindful me-time or sharing the fun with friends and family, both kits are designed to spark creativity, laughter, and pieces you’ll be proud to make (and keep!).
What’s inside?

🪨 2 x Bags of Super Clay
🙂 Tool tube (lid doubles as a water pot)
📝 1 x Pott’d quick-start guide with step-by-step instructions, and inspiration cards to create a whole bunch of creative pottery
Ultimate Starter Kit
🪨 3 x Bags of Super Clay. Each bag is 500g, giving you plenty for multiple projects.
⭕ 1 x Circular cutter. Perfect for creating the perfect circle shape.

📖 1 x Ultimate Starter Pottery Handbook, includes step-by-step instructions, tips and painting techniques.
Both kits include:
🔪 3 x Essential sculpting tools. A cutting tool, a needle tool and a scoring tool.

🧑‍🎨 8 x Pottery paint sets. Choose from Regular, Pastel, Minimalist, Cosmic, Copenhagen, or Retro.
🎨 1 x Paint palette. Features a ridged back for easy brush cleaning.
🪵 1 x Rolling pin. Comes with thickness gauges for perfectly even clay rolling.

✨ Base coat & gloss varnish (professional finishing touch)

What testing do Home Pottery Kits undergo?
All components included in the kit have been tested to ensure compliance with relevant US safety standards. All art materials conform to ASTM D-4236. Additionally, our kits have been reviewed by an independent toxicologist and carry clear warning labels and safety instructions to protect users where applicable; you'll see this labelling on every kit. 

For safest use, we recommend keeping your creations away from food, drink, excessive water and rain. Avoid swallowing clay or inhaling dust and never sand dry clay. Use a little water while working to minimise dust. Avoid contact with eyes, and rinse immediately with plenty of clean water if exposed. Also wash your hands and wipe surfaces with warm, soapy water after use.

While the kit has been designed with adults in mind, we know that getting creative together can be a fun way for families to spend quality time. For added peace of mind, the kit has been toy safety tested and is suitable for children aged 8 years and above when used under adult supervision.

If you have any questions about our materials, testing, or the safe use of the kit, we're always happy to help.
How long will my clay be happy sat on a shelf?
Our Super Clay can be stored up to 9 months - to prolong its lovely life, we advise that you store it in a cool place! We're not talking a room with funky lights and a disco ball, but somewhere with lower temperatures. Once you have received your kit, you've got a good 3/4 of a year to use all that lovely clay!
Are my creations waterproof?
No sadly, air dry clay doesn’t mix well with water - it can soften or lose its shape if it gets wet. That’s why your Starter kit includes an insert for the plant pot project, and we recommend using dried flowers for your vase. The paints and varnish do add a light layer of protection, but your pieces are best kept dry and enjoyed as decorative keepsakes.
