Project details:
Skill Level - Beginner:
Printed Design Size: 5.0x15.0 cm | 2.0x6.0 in
Canvas Size: 11.4x20.0 cm | 4.5x7.9 in
Canvas Mesh Size: 13 holes per inch
Optional Threads Included: DMC Soft Matte Cotton
Included in the "Complete Kit with Finishing Materials"
Artwork printed in premium interlock canvas, with edges sewed with cotton bias tape
2 tapestry needles size 18
Flyer with the color threads mapping so you can start stitching quickly.
Threads with enough quantity to stitch with the continental tent stitch (more information in the Product FAQ's ; if you need more thread, leave a note at checkout - we offer you all the thread needed without extra cost!)
Needlepoint booklet with instructions on starting needlepoint, tips for selecting needlepoint stitches, and a library of favorite stitches
FINISHING MATERIALS
A piece of self-adhesive velvet fabric for the backing, measuring the same size as the printed design size
Extra thread for the binding stitch .
Please refer to the image in the product gallery to check the color of the extra thread, cotton twill tape and key fob hardware.
BESPOKE PACKAGING
Our packaging is portable and sustainable & perfect for gifting. Please note that we pack everything into the same box, unless you let us know that you're buying gifts.
More details below in tab "HOW WE PACK YOUR ORDER".
Included With The "Printed Canvas only"
When you buy the “ Printed canvas only ” option, you'll receive:
Artwork printed in premium interlock canvas, with edges sewed with cotton bias tape
1 tapestry needle size 18 (or size 20, if the design is printed on 18 mesh canvas)
Flyer with the color threads mapping so you can start stitching quickly.
Needlepoint booklet with instructions on starting needlepoint, tips for selecting needlepoint stitches, and a library of favorite stitches.
It’s the perfect option if you want to create your own color variation, or re-use threads and yarn you might have at home!
FINISHING MATERIALS
In case you want to buy the supplies for self-finishing, you can add the self-adhesive velvet fabric for the backing, measuring the same size as the printed design size.
How we pack your order
SINGLE PACKAGING FOR MULTIPLE ITEMS:
To be more sustainable, we consciously try to reduce the amount of materials used in packaging.
When buying more than one kit or canvas , we will always try to fit them into a single packaging tube or box that best fits their size , and as they are for the same person we will only include 1 printed needlepoint booklet .
However, if you buy a gift wrapping or add an order note at checkout letting us know that you are buying a gift , we make sure that the kits are shipped in individual packaging .
ABOUT OUR PACKAGING:
♥ PLASTIC FREE It's the little things that matter. We're careful to use only eco-friendly materials, that you can reuse or recycle!
♥ GIFTS All our kits come beautifully packaged with our bespoke portable tubes or small boxes, making them a perfect gift for you or a friend.

Project Type: Cultural portuguese souvenir needlepoint bookmark kit.
Materials: 13-mesh Zweigart canvas and high quality DMC cotton threads.
Best For: Travelers and fans of Portuguese design or folk art style.
A tribute to the streets of Portugal , the Portuguese Sardine needlepoint kit is a vibrant piece of cultural artistry. This Unwind Studio bookmark kit features a 13-mesh printed canvas and premium DMC cotton threads , making it a meaningful, handmade souvenir for anyone who loves Mediterranean charm.
Celebrate the charm and culture of Portugal with our "Portuguese Sardine" needlepoint bookmark kit. Featuring a cute sardine adorned with some hidden Portuguese references, this kit is perfect for those who appreciate cultural artistry and meaningful souvenirs.
Every stitch tells a story, making your reading experience as rich and lively as a stroll through the streets of Porto.
Want everything in one box? The Portuguese Sardine Bookmark Starter Kit comes with the finishing materials, a mesh project bag and our Embroidery Essentials Bag, so you're ready to stitch, finish and carry your project.
