Frequently Asked Questions
Who is this kit for?
The Pottery Date Night Bundle is perfect for couples who love getting creative together, from first-time makers to seasoned artists. It’s a thoughtful gift for anniversaries, date nights, or cozy nights in.
This product is intended for adults and children over 14 years old. If you are looking for products for children, please check out our
Kids range
.
What makes the Pottery Date Night Bundle special for couples?
The bundle is designed for two, making it a fun, hands-on date night experience. You and your partner can shape, and personalize your own pottery using premium air-dry clay and included tools.
How does the clay harden, and how should I store it?
Our air-dry clay naturally hardens within 24–48 hours - no kiln needed! It contains special fibres that let it dry strong and smooth. To keep leftovers soft, store them in the resealable bag and avoid leaving the clay out too long, as it will begin to dry. Once finished, add a coat of Sculpd varnish for a durable, ceramic-like finish.
How long does it take to ship?
Shipping times depend on the delivery option you choose at checkout.
Standard shipping typically arrives within 2–3 days for most contiguous US states.
Need it sooner? Choose Express 2 Day or Express 3 Day to guarantee faster delivery.
Can I return it if I change my mind?
No problem - just get in touch and we’ll guide you through the returns process.
Please don’t send your kit to our registered address, as we won’t be able to receive or refund it there. Once your return reaches us, we’ll process your refund within 14 days.
