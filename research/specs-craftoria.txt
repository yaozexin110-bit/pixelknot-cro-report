A few things worth
knowing
What kind of photo works best?
Use a bright, sharp, high-resolution image with one clear subject. Leave a little space around faces and important details so the composition has room to breathe.
Which color count should I choose?
Choose 24 for a simpler, bolder painting; 36 for a balanced level of detail; 48 for more shading and definition; or 80 colors, available with the 50 × 60 cm canvas, for our most detailed palette with smoother blends and subtle color transitions.
What happens after I upload my photo?
Your image is converted into a numbered design and matched with a custom paint set. Because the kit is made for you, preparation happens before it enters the delivery network.
Is a frame included?
A frame is included only when you choose a framed variant. Select “No Frame” if you prefer the numbered canvas without frame components.
How do shipping, tracking, and returns work?
Tracking is sent when your order ships. Delivery timing varies by destination; see our
shipping policy
. Craftoria offers 30-day free returns; review the
return policy
for eligibility and instructions.
